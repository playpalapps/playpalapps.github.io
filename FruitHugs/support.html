Playing the game

How do I play?

Slide your finger to aim, lift it to drop the friend into the bowl. When two matching friends touch, they hug and turn into the next friend up the chain. In Classic and Daily, keep the pile below the dashed line at the top of the bowl; if friends sit above it for a few seconds the round ends and you can play again straight away.

What is the order of the fruit friends?

Blueberry, Cherry, Strawberry, Lime, Plum, Orange, Apple, Peach, Pineapple, Melon, Watermelon. Only the first five are ever dropped; the rest you grow by merging. Two Watermelons meeting is a Grand finale worth 120 points. Every other set (Sweet Treats, Ocean Pals, Cosmic Buddies, Garden Blooms, Cozy Critters) has eleven friends in the same sizes.

What is Zen mode?

Zen never ends. When the bowl gets full, the three highest friends float gently out of the bowl to make room, and you keep playing. Tap Pause and then Finish round whenever you want to bank your score and hearts.

What is the Daily Bowl?

Every player gets the same order of friends on the same day, so everyone in the family can compare scores. Playing on consecutive days builds a streak, which earns the Three Days and Full Week stickers.

What are the stars at the end?

One star for any round, two if you grew an Apple or bigger, three if you grew a Melon or a Watermelon (or the same level in another set).

What are hearts, and what is Dress up?

Every round pays hearts: one for every ten points, plus a bonus for the biggest friend you grew. Spend them in Dress up on new friend sets, scenes and bowls. Nothing in the game can be bought with real money.

What are stickers?

Little achievements such as First Hug, Combo Comet, Zen Master and Completionist. There are 21. Open Stickers from the Home screen to see them and your play statistics.

What is Top hugs?

Your ten best rounds across all modes, kept on this device only. When a round ends with a score good enough for the list, the game asks whether you want to save it with a name. Saving is optional. Open the list from the Friend book.

Sound, music and settings

How do I turn the sound, music or vibration off?

Open Settings from the Home screen, or use the Sounds and Music toggles on the Pause screen. Settings are remembered.

There is no sound at all

On iPhone, check the ring/silent switch on the side of the phone: Fruit Hugs respects it, like most games. Also check the volume and that Sounds and Music are switched on in the game.

The animations feel too lively

Turn on Calm motion in Settings: no shaking, fewer sparkles and no confetti. Fruit Hugs also follows your phone's Reduce Motion accessibility setting automatically.

The text is small

Turn on Bigger text in Settings. For left-handed children, Left-handed swaps the pause button and the Next preview.

How do I pause?

Tap the pause button at the top. On Android, the back button also pauses. The game pauses by itself if you switch apps or get a call.

Privacy and data

Does Fruit Hugs collect anything?

No. The game has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and it does not use the internet. The only things saved are your progress and settings, on the device. See the privacy policy.

How do I reset everything?

Open Settings and tap Reset all progress; the game asks you to confirm. That removes hearts, unlocks, stickers, the Friend book, Top hugs and settings. Uninstalling the app does the same.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Not at the moment. Progress lives only on the device, which is also why nothing about you is ever stored anywhere else.

Purchases and refunds

What does Fruit Hugs cost?

Fruit Hugs is a one-time purchase on the App Store and Google Play. You pay once and the whole game is yours: every mode, every friend set, scene and bowl.

Is there anything to buy inside the game?

No. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no ads. Every friend set, scene and bowl is unlocked with hearts earned by playing.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds go through them. On iPhone and iPad use reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android use the Google Play order history at play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory.
