How to play

One tap. That is the whole game.

Simple enough for a four-year-old, satisfying enough that grown-ups keep stealing the phone.

1

Tap to drop

Slide to aim, tap to drop a friend into the bowl. A soft ghost shows exactly where it will land.

2

Match two the same

When two matching friends touch, they hug and pop into the next one up. Two blueberries make a cherry, two cherries make a strawberry.

3

Grow the big ones

Keep merging up the chain toward the pineapple, the melon and the mighty watermelon. Every hug earns hearts to dress the game up.

Three ways to play

Pick your pace

🍓

Classic

Keep the bowl from overflowing and chase your best score. Three stars for the biggest friends.

🍃

Zen

No game over, ever. When the bowl fills up, the top friends float gently away to make room. Pure relaxation.

📅

Daily Bowl

Everyone gets the same bowl each day. Come back tomorrow to keep your streak going.

For grown-ups

Made for parents to relax about

We built Fruit Hugs the way we would want a game for our own kids. Made for Kids on the App Store, Designed for Families on Google Play.

No ads, ever

Nothing to tap by mistake, nothing to watch, nothing to buy. Unlocks are earned by playing.

Plays offline

Everything is on the device, so it works on a plane, in the car, or anywhere else.

No accounts

Nothing to sign up for. Scores, hearts and stickers stay on the device. Read the privacy policy.

Calm by design

Zen mode has no game over. Calm-motion, bigger text, left-handed layout and reduced motion are all respected.
