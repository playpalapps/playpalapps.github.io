Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026 · Last updated: 3 October 2026 · Developer: Naveen Raj (Playpal Apps) · App: Fruit Hugs (com.playpalapps.fruithugs) for iOS and Android

Fruit Hugs does not collect any personal data. Nothing leaves your device. The app never connects to the internet. This page says that in more words.

The short version: no data collection, no internet, no ads, no accounts, no tracking, no purchases. The only things saved are your game progress and settings, on your own device, and they are deleted when you uninstall the app.

1. What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information of any kind, from children or adults.
  • We do not use the internet. The app works fully offline and the Android version requests no permissions at all.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting, tracking or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising software.
  • We do not offer accounts, logins, sign-ups or social features.
  • We do not sell or share any data, because we have none.
  • We do not contain in-app purchases or links to outside websites.

2. What is stored on your device

Fruit Hugs keeps a small amount of game data in the app's own private storage on your phone or tablet:

  • Your best scores and your local Top hugs list
  • Which friends you have discovered in each set, for the Friend book
  • Hearts earned and spent, and which friend sets, scenes and bowls you have unlocked
  • Stickers earned, play statistics and your Daily Bowl streak
  • Whether you have finished the tutorial
  • Your settings: sound, music, haptics, aim guide, calm motion, left-handed layout, bigger text

This data never leaves the device and is not linked to you in any way. Deleting the app removes it completely.

3. Names in the Top hugs list

After a game, a player can choose to save their score with a name. That name is only ever stored on the device, alongside the score, so it can be shown in the app's own Top hugs list. It is never sent anywhere, and it is deleted when the app is uninstalled. Any word can be typed; it does not need to be a real name.

4. Children's privacy

Fruit Hugs is designed for children aged 4 to 8. Because the app collects no data at all, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States, the GDPR and its rules for children (GDPR-K) in the European Union and United Kingdom, and the Google Play Families policy. No parental consent is needed because no data is collected.

5. Third-party software

The game is packaged with Capacitor, an open-source framework that lets a web game run as a native app. Capacitor makes no network calls in Fruit Hugs and collects no data. The Fredoka typeface is bundled inside the app under the SIL Open Font License; it is not loaded from the internet.

6. Your rights

Because Fruit Hugs never sends data to a server, there is no database from which to retrieve, correct or delete your information. Your data lives on your device, under your control. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it saved.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Because the app has no internet access, a change would only ever come with an app update you choose to install.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email playpalapps2024@gmail.com. We reply personally, usually within seven days.
