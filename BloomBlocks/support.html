Playing the game

How do I play?

Drag any of the three pieces from the tray onto the 8×8 board. Fill a whole row or a whole column and it clears. When the tray is empty you get three new pieces. The game ends when no piece in the tray fits.

How do combos work?

Clearing lines on back-to-back turns builds a combo, and each step of the combo multiplies your line points. The combo survives up to two placements without a clear, so you have room to set up the next one.

What is the Bloom meter and the Petal Bomb?

Every line you clear fills the Bloom meter. Six lines earn a Petal Bomb, and you can hold up to three. Tap the flower button, then tap the board, to clear a 3×3 area. If no piece fits but you still have a bomb, the game keeps going so you can use it.

What is Daily Bloom?

Once a day there is a new puzzle where every player starts with the same pieces. Playing it on consecutive days builds your streak. A new puzzle arrives at midnight in your local time.

How does undo work?

You get one free undo in each game. After that an undo costs 50 petals. If you are out of petals, you can watch a reward video for a free one.

What is a revive?

Once per game, when you run out of moves, you can watch a reward video to clear four lines and get a fresh set of pieces.

What are petals for?

Petals unlock themes. You earn them from your score, from cleared lines, from achievements and from a small gift each day you open the game.

Ads and purchases

When will I see ads?

Reward videos only play when you choose them. A short ad may appear between games, never in the middle of one, and never in your first few games.

What does Remove Ads do?

It is a single one-time purchase that removes the ads between games for good. Reward videos stay available for when you want a revive, an undo or bonus petals.

I bought Remove Ads but still see ads between games.

Open Settings, tap Remove Ads, then tap Restore purchase. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it. If that does not fix it, email us.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Install the game, open the Remove Ads screen from the home screen or Settings, and tap Restore purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google, so refunds follow their policies. On Apple devices visit reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android open your order in Google Play and choose Request a refund.

Progress and settings

Is my progress backed up?

Progress is stored on your device only. It is included in your normal device backup, but it does not sync between devices, and uninstalling the game removes it.

I hear no sound.

On iPhone, check the silent switch on the side of the phone: the game respects it. Then check that Sound effects and Music are on in Settings.

How do I change the tracking or privacy choice I made?

On iPhone and iPad go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. In regions that require consent, a Privacy choices button appears in the game's Settings. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I start over?

Open Settings in the game and tap Reset all progress. This cannot be undone. A Remove Ads purchase is kept.
