The games

Ten little games, one big rainbow

Each game looks at colours a different way: finding, popping, sorting, mixing, matching and making. They start very easy and grow with the child.

Balloon Pop

Sort It

A yellow banana, a green frog. Drag each one into the basket of its colour.

Hungry Pip

How it teaches

Built for children who cannot read yet

A two-year-old should be able to pick it up and play alone. Everything in Colour Wise is designed around that.

1

Hear it

Pip says every question aloud, and names each colour as it is found. Tap Pip to hear it again, as many times as you like.

2

Try it

A wrong tap never ends a game. The blob just wobbles, Pip says which colour it was, and after two tries the right one gives a little glow.

3

Celebrate it

Five stars finish a round with confetti and a new sticker for the sticker book. There are forty to collect.

Look and feel

Bright, soft and friendly

Big targets for little fingers, a sunny sky, and nothing that flashes or shouts. Swipe to see more.
