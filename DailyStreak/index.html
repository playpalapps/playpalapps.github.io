How it works

One tap, once a day

There is nothing to fill in and nothing to rate. You did the thing or you did not, and the grid keeps count.

1

Add a habit

Pick one of 25 ready-made templates or write your own. Give it a name, one of 60 icons and one of 8 colours.

2

Check in

Tap the button on the habit's card when you have done it. A ring at the top of the screen shows how many habits are done today.

3

Watch the grid fill in

Each check-in colours a square and adds to the streak. Open a habit to see your best streak, completion rates and a month calendar.

Streaks and schedules

A streak that fits your week

Not every habit is a daily one. Choose how often each habit is due, and the streak counts only what you asked of yourself.

Every day
The classic. Check in each day and the streak grows by one.
Times a week
Set a target from 1 to 6 times a week, on any days you like. The streak then counts weeks instead of days.
Set days PRO
Choose specific days of the week. Days you did not pick are skipped and never break the streak.
Streak freeze PRO
Two a month. Use one to protect a streak across a missed day.

The details

Small things, done properly

The parts that help you keep going, without the app getting in the way.

Reminders that know when to stop

An optional reminder for each habit at a time you choose, plus one evening nudge at 9 pm only if habits are still unchecked. It stops by itself after a week away.

Home screen widgets

Keep the streak for your first habit on your home screen. Small and medium widgets on iPhone and iPad, and a small widget on Android.

Share a streak

Turn a streak into an image card, in square or story format, or share a year-in-review card. Milestones get a short celebration along the way.

Gentle mode

If streak numbers feel like pressure, switch to gentle mode and see a completion percentage instead. You can also add a short note to a check-in.

Back up and restore

Save everything to a file you keep, and restore it on a new phone. Both are free. Pro adds CSV export for spreadsheets.

Light and dark

Dark by default, with a light theme too. Sounds and haptics can each be switched off.
