The collection

Four objects, each on its own paper

Swipe up and down to move between them. Every one is lit like a product photo, and each answers your thumb the way the real thing would.

F-01

Panel

One graphite object with six faces. Swipe sideways and it turns over to the next.

  • Switch
  • Pop
  • Click
  • Slide
  • Roll

F-02

Keys

A switch tester with nine keys in three rows: clicky, tactile and linear. Each row sounds like its own keyboard, because it is one.

F-03

Cradle

Five steel balls. Pull one out and let go: the knock travels down the row and the far ball swings out, until it slowly comes to rest.

F-04

Coin

Flick a brass coin into a spin and listen to it settle, faster and faster, until it rings flat. Tap it to stop it.

The details

Made to feel right

A fidget lives or dies by how it feels. These are the parts you notice with your thumb.

Strong haptics

Every detent, click and knock has its own haptic, at full strength. Turn them down in Settings if you like them softer.

Real sound

Recordings of real switches, keyboards and metal, kept soft. The coin rings, and the cradle clacks.

Real physics

The cradle passes its knock down the row, the coin spins down like the real thing, and a steel ball rolls where you tilt.

Light that follows your day

Cool in the morning, golden in the late afternoon, and a warm desk lamp at night.

Five finishes

Graphite, Silver, Sage, Cobalt or Sand. Tap a sample coin on the desk to change them all.

Kind to your battery

When nothing is moving, Fidgety stops drawing and rests, and wakes the moment you touch it.

Private by default

No account. No tracking. No ads.

Fidgety has no sign-up, no analytics and no advertising, and it makes no network requests. Read the privacy policy.
