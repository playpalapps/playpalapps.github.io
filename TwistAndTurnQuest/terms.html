3. The game itself

Twist and Turn Quest is a single-player, offline-first casual maze game. There are no leaderboards beyond your device's local top-10, no multiplayer, no social features, no chat, and no user-to-user communication of any kind. The game tilts a ball through procedurally generated mazes using your device's accelerometer; that is the entire surface area you interact with.

The game contains seven modes (Quest, Time Quest, Beacon, Echo, Hunt, Constellation, PRISM) plus a Daily Challenge and a 2D Classic mode preserving the original-release feel. 10. Motion sickness, accessibility, and physical safety

Twist and Turn Quest is a tilt-controlled game. A small number of players experience motion sickness, eye strain, or headaches from the camera following the rolling ball — especially in 3D modes (Quest, Time Quest, Beacon, Echo, Hunt, Constellation, PRISM). If you experience any discomfort, take a break or switch to the 2D Classic mode, which is camera-fixed.

The game requires physical movement of your device. Please don't play while walking, cycling, driving, or operating any vehicle or machinery, and please give yourself enough space that swinging the phone around can't damage anything or anyone nearby.
