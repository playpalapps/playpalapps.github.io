Modes

Nine ways to play.

One tilt-maze idea, refracted nine ways. Switch the moment Quest gets too zen, or PRISM gets too neon.

Mode 01 · Core

Quest

The classic. Roll to the destination. Endless levels, increasing maze complexity. Where everyone starts and where most return.

Mode 02 · Chase

Time Quest

A creeping red corridor reveals the deadly path five seconds in, completing in 200. Outrun it or die rolling.

Mode 03 · Find

Beacon

The destination is invisible until you find it. The maze pulses dimly; the beacon glows brighter the closer you roll.

Mode 04 · Memory

Echo

The maze flashes for a heartbeat, then fades. Hold the layout in your head and roll from memory. Brutally elegant.

Mode 05 · Reflex

Hunt

Chase the wisp. Catch one, then the next, and the next. Chain them without bumping a wall for combo glow.

Mode 06 · Pattern

Constellation

Touch the stars in numerical order. Wrong order shakes the screen; right order draws a line across a night sky.

NEW
Mode 07 · Hypnotic

PRISM

Round mazes on a polar grid. Every round, a different shape and ring count. The mode you'll show your friends.

Mode 08 · Daily

Daily Challenge

One curated level per day, the same level for every player worldwide. Resets at midnight in your local time.

Mode 09 · Classic

2D Classic

The camera-fixed original mode. No 3D camera follow, no motion-sickness risk. Pure top-down tilt-and-roll.

Why this one

Built around one good tilt.

No accounts. No nagging notifications. No surprise paywalls. Just a phone, your wrist, and a maze that doesn't lie about its physics.

Offline-first

No accounts, no servers, no cloud sync. Your progress lives on your device. Airplane mode? Still rolls.

Honest pacing

One interstitial ad every third level. Never on retry. Never in your first session. That's the whole rhythm.

Real physics

Continuous-collision-detected RigidBody3D. The ball won't tunnel through walls at extreme tilt. We checked.

2D Classic fallback

If 3D camera-follow makes you queasy, the original top-down view is one tap away. Motion sickness, solved.

Daily Challenge

One curated level per day. Same level for every player worldwide. Compete with yourself or just enjoy the ritual.

Earn the skins

Five ball skins — Cyan, Gold, Emerald, Crimson, Aurora. Unlocked by stars, not by IAP. The shinier the ball, the more you played.

Tilt your way out.

Free on iOS and Android. Same builds across iPhone, iPad, and Android phones & tablets. Portrait orientation, optimised for one-handed play.
