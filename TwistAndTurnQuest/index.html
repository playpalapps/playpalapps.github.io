Modes

Seven ways to play.

One tilt-maze idea, refracted seven ways. Switch the moment Quest gets too zen, or PRISM gets too neon.

Quest

The core mode. Roll to the destination. Endless levels, increasing maze complexity. Where everyone starts.

Time Quest

A creeping red corridor reveals the deadly path five seconds in, completing in 200. Outrun it.

Beacon

The destination is invisible until you find it. The maze pulses dimly; the beacon glows brighter the closer you get.

Echo

You see the maze briefly when you start, then it fades. Hold the layout in your head and roll from memory.

Hunt

Chase the wisp. Catch one, then the next. Chain them without bumping a wall for combo glow.

Constellation

Touch the stars in numerical order. Wrong order shakes the screen; right order draws a line across the sky.

NEW

PRISM

Round mazes on a polar grid. Every round, a different shape and ring count. The most visually distinctive mode.

Daily Challenge

One curated level per day, the same for every player worldwide. Resets at midnight in your local time.

2D Classic

The camera-fixed original-feel mode. No 3D camera follow, no motion-sickness risk. Pure top-down tilt-and-roll.
