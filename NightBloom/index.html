Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone/iPad: Go to Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Night Bloom → Cancel Subscription. On Android: Open Google Play Store → Menu → Subscriptions → Night Bloom → Cancel. You'll retain access until the end of your current billing period.

Can I restore my purchase on a new device?

Yes! Open Night Bloom, go to the Premium screen, and tap "Restore Purchases." Your subscription is tied to your Apple ID or Google account and will be restored automatically.

How does the AI Story Teller work?

Choose a theme and tap "Tell Me a Story." Our AI generates a unique, calming bedtime story just for you. Each story is different — no two are ever the same. You get up to 10 stories per day.

Does Night Bloom work offline?

Soundscapes, breathing exercises, and games work offline. AI-generated stories require an internet connection. Pre-written stories are available offline.

What permissions does the app need?

Microphone access is optional — used only in the Night Bloom game to detect if you make noise. Motion sensors are used for the "hold still" game mechanic. No personal data is collected or shared.

Is my data private?

Night Bloom does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All preferences are stored locally on your device. We don't use analytics or tracking services.

Overview

Night Bloom ("we", "our", "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

The short version: We collect virtually no personal data. Your preferences stay on your device. We don't track you, sell your data, or use third-party analytics.

Information We Collect

Information we do NOT collect

  • Names, email addresses, or account information
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, or files
  • Browsing history or usage analytics
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Audio recordings (microphone data is processed locally and never stored or transmitted)

Information stored locally on your device

  • App preferences (sound volumes, selected themes, onboarding status)
  • Subscription status (verified via Apple/Google)
  • Game progress and streak data

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

AI Story Teller

When you use the AI Story Teller feature, your selected theme is sent to a third-party AI service to generate a story. No personal information is included in these requests. Generated stories are not stored on our servers.

Purchase information

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We do not receive or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing address. We only receive confirmation of whether a valid subscription exists.

Device Permissions

  • Microphone — Used only in the Night Bloom game to detect ambient noise levels. Audio is processed in real-time on your device and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.
  • Motion sensors — Used only in the Night Bloom game to detect device movement. Sensor data is processed locally and never stored or transmitted.
  • Internet — Required for AI story generation, subscription verification, and loading web fonts.

All permissions are optional. The app functions without them (the game features that use microphone/motion will be limited).

Third-Party Services

  • Apple App Store / Google Play Store — For subscription management and purchase processing.
  • AI service provider — For generating bedtime stories. Only the selected theme is transmitted; no personal data is shared.
  • Google Fonts — Font files may be loaded from Google's servers on the support website (not within the app).

We do not use any analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Night Bloom does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app does not require account creation or collect any personal data. Parents and guardians should supervise children when using the AI Story Teller feature, as content is generated automatically.

Data Retention

Since we don't collect personal data, there is nothing to retain. All app data is stored locally on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Your Rights

You can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling Night Bloom from your device. Since we don't collect or store personal data on our servers, there is no data to request, export, or delete from our side.
