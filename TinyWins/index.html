How it works

Small on purpose

It is not a to-do list. Nothing here is waiting to be done. You only ever write down what already went well.

1

Tap and type

Open the app, tap Log a win and write a few words. Suggestions learn the wins you log most, so the usual ones are one tap away.

2

Watch it add up

Each day you log a win adds to your streak. The week fills in, dot by dot, on the card at the top of the screen.

3

Look back

Every win is kept in your journal, day by day. On a flat day, scroll back and see how much you actually did.

Streaks that forgive

One off day does not undo the rest

Most streaks punish you for being human. This one banks a rest day every time you log seven days in a row, and spends it for you if you miss a day.

  • Up to two rest days kept in reserve
  • A missed day uses one, and your streak carries on
  • Forgot last night? Add a win to yesterday
  • If a streak does end, the app says "a fresh start", not "you failed"

The details

Quiet, and on your side

The parts that make it something you come back to, without it nagging you to.

Insights

Wins over the last 7 days, 30 days and 12 weeks, where they come from, and gentle patterns such as your strongest day of the week.

One gentle reminder

Optional, at a time you choose. It is skipped once you have logged a win, and it goes quiet after a week away instead of piling up.

Share the good stuff

Turn your day or a milestone into a clean image card and send it wherever you like.

Mood alongside wins

See your check-ins next to each day in the journal, and whether you tend to feel better on days with more wins.

Light and dark

Follows your device, or pick one. Haptic feedback can be switched off.

Your data is yours

Export everything as CSV or JSON whenever you like, or erase it all from Settings.

Private by default

No account. No tracking. No ads.

Your wins, check-ins and notes are stored on your device and nowhere else. Tiny Wins has no sign-up, no analytics and no advertising, and it has no server to send anything to. Read the privacy policy.
