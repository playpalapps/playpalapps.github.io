The details

Your AI, your keys, your photos

Postela has no server of its own. What you make stays with you.

Bring your own AI

Connect Groq, Claude or OpenAI with your own API key and Postela writes your copy in seconds. Groq has a free tier.

Or write it yourself

No key, no problem. In Local mode your own tagline becomes the headline and your description the caption.

Keys stay on the device

API keys are kept in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore and are sent only to the provider you chose.

Photos never upload

Your photos are used on the device to build the layout. Only the text you type is sent to an AI provider, and only if you use one.

3D device tilt

Flat, tilted left or tilted right, with a real perspective render and a soft shadow under the phone.

History

Your last 50 posts are kept on the device, so you can reopen one and export it again.
