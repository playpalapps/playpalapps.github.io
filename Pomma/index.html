The rhythm

Four sprints, then a proper rest

This is one full cycle at Pomma's starting settings, drawn to scale: about two hours of work with the breaks already built in.

Choose one task

Before a session starts, Pomma asks what you are working on. It is a small pause that makes the next 25 minutes about one thing.

Focus until the bell

The ring counts down and the screen stays awake. If you leave the app, a notification tells you when the session ends.

Take the break

Five minutes away from the work. After four sessions the break is a long one. Then you begin again.

The details

Small things, done properly

Begin with intent

An optional sheet before each session: pick the task, confirm the length, note your mood. Turn it off if you would rather just press start.

A daily goal

Set how many sessions make a good day. Pomma counts them, marks the day when you get there, and keeps your streak.

Sounds you choose

Four alarms: Bell, Chime, Digital and Gentle. A soft or classic tick if you like one, silence if you do not. Haptics and volume are yours too.

On your Lock Screen iOS

A Live Activity shows the running session on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, so you never unlock just to check the time.

Widgets iOS

Home Screen widgets in two sizes, and Lock Screen widgets, show today's sessions against your goal.

Light and dark

Follow the system or choose for yourself. Every palette is tuned for both.
