Privacy Policy

Gramam, by Playpal Apps. Last updated October 2026.

Gramam collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that send data anywhere.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress in the game: the day in the Malayalam calendar, the diary, the pantry, letters, and settings such as sound and hints. It is stored only in the app’s local storage on your device.
  • Deleting the app deletes this data. “Tap and hold the title to start over” also erases it.

What we never do

  • We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any information about you.
  • The app does not use the network. It works fully offline.
  • The app does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Children

Gramam is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
