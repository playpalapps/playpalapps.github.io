Playing the game

How do I play?

Slide your finger to aim, lift it to drop the veggie into the jar. When two matching veggies touch, they hug and turn into the next veggie up the chain. Keep the pile below the dashed line at the top of the jar. If veggies sit above the line for a few seconds, the harvest ends and you can play again straight away.

What is the order of the veggies?

Pea, Radish, Tomato, Mushroom, Potato, Bell pepper, Eggplant, Broccoli, Cauliflower, Pumpkin. Only the first five are ever dropped; the rest you grow by merging. Two Pumpkins meeting is a Pumpkin party worth 100 points.

What are the stars at the end?

One star for any harvest, two if you grew an Eggplant or bigger, three if you grew a Cauliflower or a Pumpkin.

What is the Veggie Book?

It is your collection. Every new veggie you grow for the first time is added with a "New friend!" banner. Open it from the Home screen to see everyone you have found.

What is Top harvests?

Your ten best games, kept on this device only. When a game ends with a score good enough for the list, the game asks whether you want to save it with a name. Saving is optional. Open the list from the Veggie Book.

Sound, music and settings

How do I turn the sound or music off?

Tap the Sounds or Music toggle on the Home screen or on the Pause screen. The setting is remembered.

There is no sound at all

On iPhone, check the ring/silent switch on the side of the phone: Veggie Pop respects it, like most games. Also check the volume and that Sounds and Music are switched on in the game.

Does it have a dark mode?

Yes. Veggie Pop follows your phone's light or dark setting automatically.

The animations feel too lively

Turn on Reduce Motion in your phone's accessibility settings. Veggie Pop then calms its bobbing, shaking and sparkle effects.

How do I pause?

Tap the pause button in the top left. On Android, the back button also pauses. The game pauses by itself if you switch apps or get a call.

Privacy and data

Does Veggie Pop collect anything?

No. The game has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and it does not use the internet. The only things saved are your progress and settings, on the device. See the privacy policy.

How do I reset everything?

Uninstall the app and install it again. That removes the best score, the Veggie Book, the Top harvests list and the settings.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Not at the moment. Progress lives only on the device, which is also why nothing about you is ever stored anywhere else.

Purchases and refunds

Is there anything to buy inside the game?

No. There are no in-app purchases and no ads.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds go through them. On iPhone and iPad use reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android use the Google Play order history at play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory.
