One tap. That is the whole game.

Simple enough for a four-year-old, satisfying enough that grown-ups keep stealing the phone.

1

Tap to drop

Slide to aim, tap to drop a veggie into the glass jar. It tumbles, bounces and settles with a soft little squish.

2

Match two the same

When two matching veggies touch, they hug and pop into the next one up. Two peas make a radish, two radishes make a tomato.

3

Grow the big ones

Keep merging up the chain toward the broccoli, the cauliflower and the mighty pumpkin. Fill the jar past the rim and the harvest is over. Play again!
