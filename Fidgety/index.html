The details

Made to feel right

A fidget toy lives or dies by how it feels. These are the parts you notice with your thumb.

Haptics everywhere

A light tick for a notch, a thud for a pop, a pulse while you squeeze. Switch them off in Settings if you would rather not.

Real momentum

The spinner coasts and slows by itself, beads carry their speed into each other, and the marble rolls where you tilt.

Favorites

Mark the fidgets you reach for most and they wait at the top of the home screen.

Light and dark

Follows your device, or pick one. Pro adds four accent colours.

Works offline

Nothing to load and nothing to sign in to. Open it and start fidgeting.

Nothing leaves your device

Your settings, favorites and tap counts stay on your phone. There is no account and no analytics.
