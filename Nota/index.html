What goes in a note

Text, lists, drawings and photos

A note can hold any mix of them, and there are a few ways to keep notes organised and find them again.

Rich text

Headings, bold, italic, underline and strikethrough, lists, quotes, code blocks, links and dividers.

Checklists

Tick items off as you go. A count shows how many are done.

Sketches

Draw inside a note with a pen, pencil, highlighter and eraser. Pick a colour and stroke width, and undo or redo.

Photos

Add a photo from your library, or take one with the camera.

Folders and tags

Sort notes into folders, label them with coloured tags, and pin the ones you need to the top.

Search

Search across all your notes, and filter by type, tags and date.

Reminders

Set a reminder on any note and get a notification at the time you pick.

Locked notes

Keep a note behind Face ID, your fingerprint or the device passcode.

The details

Your notes are yours to keep and move

Because your notes exist only on your device, Nota gives you ways to recover them, back them up and take them elsewhere.

Version history

When you edit a note, its previous text is kept. Open the history to read an earlier version or restore it.

Archive and trash

Archive notes you are done with. Deleted notes stay in the trash for 30 days before they are removed.

Backup and restore

Save everything to a single file, keep it wherever you like, and restore from it on this device or another one.

Share and export

Share a note as text, PDF or Markdown. Export all your notes as a ZIP of Markdown files.

Themes and colours

Light and dark themes with 12 accent colours, and a background colour for any note.

iPad and tablets

Layouts made for larger screens on iPad and Android tablets.

Private by default

No account. No server. No tracking.

Nota has no sign-up, no analytics and no advertising. Your notes are stored only on your device, and Playpal Apps receives nothing. There is no sync between devices; to move your notes, make a backup file and restore it. Read the privacy policy.
